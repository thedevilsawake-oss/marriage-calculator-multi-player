        How scoring works
        • Each opponent pays the winner 3 if they saw the joker, 10 if they did not.
        • If the winner ended with 8 dublees, each opponent pays an extra 5.
        • Players who did not see the joker score 0 maal.
        • Everyone settles the difference in maal with everyone else. The app does this through the winner: each player pays T + w − n × S, where T is the total maal, S is that player's maal, n is the number of players and w is their 3/10 (+5) payment. A negative amount means the winner pays them.
